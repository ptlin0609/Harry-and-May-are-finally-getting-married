場地資訊 Venue & Getting There

Royal Hotel ChiaoHsi

礁溪老爺酒店・宜蘭縣礁溪鄉五峰路69號 Open in Google Maps ↗ GETTING THERE交通方式

Arriving at Taoyuan International Airport? Take the Airport MRT to Taipei Main Station first.若您從桃園國際機場抵達，請先搭乘機場捷運至台北車站。

  • By bus Direct coaches run from Taipei to the ChiaoHsi (Jiaoxi) Transfer Station in about 50–70 minutes.
    • Kamalan Bus 1915 — departs from Taipei Bus Station, a short walk from Taipei Main Station.
    • Capital Bus 1572 — departs from City Hall Metro Station, Exit 2 (Blue Line to City Hall).
    From the ChiaoHsi (Jiaoxi) Transfer Station, it's a short taxi ride to the hotel — or a 10-minute walk to ChiaoHsi (Jiaoxi) Train Station to catch the hotel's free shuttle (see shuttle schedule). 直達客運從台北出發，約50–70分鐘可抵達礁溪轉運站。
    • 葛瑪蘭客運1915——於台北轉運站發車，從台北車站步行即可抵達。
    • 首都客運1572——於市政府捷運站2號出口發車（搭乘捷運藍線至市政府站）。
    抵達礁溪轉運站後，可搭乘計程車快速抵達飯店，或步行約10分鐘至礁溪火車站搭乘飯店免費接駁車（查看接駁時刻表）。
  • By train Take the train to ChiaoHsi (Jiaoxi) Station — the hotel runs a free shuttle for guests (see shuttle schedule) or it's a quick taxi ride. 搭乘火車至礁溪火車站（查看接駁時刻表），或可搭乘計程車前往。
  • By car / taxi From Taipei or Taoyuan International Airport, take National Freeway 5 through the Xueshan Tunnel, exit at Toucheng, then follow Provincial Highway 9 toward ChiaoHsi / Wufengqi Scenic Area — about 5 minutes further to the hotel. 從台北或桃園國際機場出發，經國道五號通過雪山隧道，於頭城交流道下交流道，接台九線往礁溪／五峰旗風景區方向，再約5分鐘即可抵達飯店。
